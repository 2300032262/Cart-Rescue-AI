About Cart Rescue AI

An AI-powered platform designed to predict cart abandonment, understand customer intent, recommend the right intervention, and help businesses recover lost revenue.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Turning Abandoned Carts Into Recovered Revenue

Cart Rescue AI is a smart e-commerce intelligence platform created to address one of the most important challenges in online shopping: cart abandonment.

Instead of treating every abandoned cart in the same way, the system analyzes customer behaviour and attempts to determine the reason behind a potential abandonment.

Based on these behavioural signals, the AI engine generates a risk score and recommends an appropriate recovery strategy.

🧠

Understand Before You Intervene

The platform focuses on identifying customer intent before recommending a recovery action.

THE PROBLEM

Why Cart Abandonment Matters

Customers may leave the checkout process for completely different reasons. A single recovery strategy cannot effectively address every situation.

01
💳

Payment Problems

A failed payment can cause a high-intent customer to leave even when they still want the product.

02
🚚

Shipping Costs

Unexpected or expensive shipping charges can create friction at the final stage of checkout.

03
💰

Price Concerns

Customers may compare prices or hesitate before committing to a purchase.

04
🧩

Checkout Friction

Complicated checkout experiences can cause customers to abandon the process.
