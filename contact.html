FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cart Rescue AI? +

Cart Rescue AI is an intelligent e-commerce platform designed to predict cart abandonment, identify potential reasons and recommend recovery actions.

How does the AI prediction work? +

The system analyzes behavioural signals such as cart value, session activity, checkout behaviour, payment status and previous interactions to calculate an abandonment risk score.

Can the system identify why a cart was abandoned? +

Yes. The AI engine categorizes likely causes such as payment failure, shipping cost, price concerns, checkout friction and product comparison.

Does the AI always recommend discounts? +

No. The recommendation engine attempts to select the most appropriate recovery action. A discount is only one possible strategy.

Can I view analytics and reports? +

Yes. The Analytics and Reports sections provide business performance metrics, recovery trends, customer insights and AI performance information.
